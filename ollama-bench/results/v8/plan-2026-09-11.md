# v8 campaign plan of record

*Written 2026-09-11 by the Claude Code control session. Owner approved items
1-6 of the six-item list and granted **20 hours of RTX 5080 GPU time**.
Nothing here is built yet; no GPU time has been spent.*

## 1. What v8 is, and why it is not v7 harder

v7 is saturated and does not discriminate: 19/20 against a ~50% target, and
across three quants UDQ3KXL 20/20, IQ2_M 19/20, Q2_K 19/20 with the two misses
on disjoint rows — "the signature of trial noise, not of a difficulty gradient"
(`results/v7/decisions-r5-2026-09-06.md`). Reference arms sat at 90-100%, so
there is no desaturation either end.

v8 therefore stops asking *how capable is this quant* and asks *does the
deployed leaf do the jobs it has been given*. That follows the owner's ruling
of 2026-09-06 — "the local model's capability is settled and the question is
integration, not capability" — and the fact that since 2026-09-11
`local/q27-IQ2_M-96k` is slbh's **default leaf agent**
(`org/local-ollama-route-2026-09-11.md`).

So v8 is an **acceptance instrument, not a ranking instrument**. A ceiling is
an acceptable result for a cell here; what is not acceptable is a cell that
cannot say go or no-go for a use. One model is scored: `q27-IQ2_M-96k` at
`num_ctx` 98304, the tag that is actually live.

## 2. GPU budget

The accounted quantity is GPU-holding time, as in `results/v7/GPU_BUDGET.log`.
Budget: **20 h = 72,000 s**. Planning units come from v7's measured 10-trial
cells, which cost 560-3,399 s (`results/v7/GPU_BUDGET.log`): light 600 s,
standard 1,800 s, heavy 3,100 s.

The reserve is sized from what v7 actually burned after its first pass: two
full re-run cells, one Windows subprocess stall needing a resume, and an n=20
extension. Spending priority, in order: (a) re-runs and stall resumes;
(b) n=20 on any cell whose Wilson interval straddles its decision threshold;
(c) the Track A shapes — long serial state and large correct output — if the
owner folds them in. A hard stop and report at **18 h accounted**; the last
2 h are never planned work.

Reference arms and all authoring run on paid plans and cost no GPU.

## 3. The six items

**Item 1 — leaf-loop fidelity under slbh's tool surface.** slbh gives a leaf
about nineteen tools (`glob`, `grep`, `read_file`, `read_bytes`, `read_lines`,
`edit_file`, `apply_patch`, `write_file`, `quick_bash`, `long_job`,
`list_jobs`, `read_job`, `kill_job`, `quick_py`, `long_py`,
`list_subagents`, `launch_subagent`, `msg_subagent`, `end_subagent`). Nothing
has ever tested this model in that role. The runner lifts slbh's tool schemas
**verbatim from slbh's own source** and drives them from a thin Python loop
against the Ollama endpoint, executing them in a sandbox. It measures slbh's
tool surface, not slbh's runtime, and says so.

Scored per trial, from the loop's own JSONL: valid-call rate, schema
violations, wrong-tool rate, narration-instead-of-call, recovery after one
injected tool error, turns to done, and task success. Six tasks: locate and
report; a byte-exact `apply_patch` edit; run a command and act on its output;
a `long_job` poll to completion; an injected tool-error recovery; a multi-file
consistency edit.

**Item 2 — near-window input, with occupancy guaranteed by construction.**
The unmeasured boundary: v7's main band carried 29-36k of material but
`peak_prompt` was only 3,030-17,376 tokens, 4-26% of the window, because "the
model now sets the material aside by never opening it" (D7-32). The routing
rule's 80k input cap is therefore "a precaution, not a measured boundary"
(`org/local-workhorse-plan-2026-09-06.md` §5).

Fix: the material is delivered **in the prompt** and is **load-bearing**.
This is not the withdrawn synthetic fill. That fill was non-load-bearing
padding added to reach a token count; here every block can carry a needle and
the answer is an aggregate across blocks, so nothing can be skipped without
losing the answer — which is exactly D7-32's own requirement that "a band that
is meant to be occupied has to be built so the *answer* cannot be assembled
from a handful of targeted reads". *Flagged for the owner as the one place v8
re-opens a withdrawn mechanism, under a different definition.*

Two shapes: multi-needle aggregation where the answer is a computed aggregate
(so no single retrieval suffices), and three-source contradiction
reconciliation. Three rungs at 20k / 50k / 80k of the 96k window, bracketing
the 80k cap, with needle depth stratified across 10/30/50/70/90% positions.
Strict-JSON answers, graded exactly.

**Item 3 — seeded read-and-report, on the three ranked production uses.**
Grading is mechanical because the ground truth is planted. Use A: summarise a
real report with K planted claims and M decoys; score claim recall, decoy
omission, and hallucination as any number or identifier absent from the
source. Use B: contradiction hunt across a pair of org pages with K real
contradictions and L near-misses that only look like contradictions — scored
as precision and recall over a known set, which is the real question (is the
candidate list cheap enough to adjudicate). Use C: changelog from a git range,
where every claim must map to a commit touching that path. Two occupancy rungs
each.

**Item 4 — abstention as a scored axis.** v7 put confidently-wrong at 0.031
[0.010, 0.086] and that number is the whole reason for "never the last
reader". Unanswerable items — fact absent, question underdetermined — are
seeded into items 2 and 3, plus two dedicated cells. Score
`correct - k*confidently_wrong` with abstention neutral, and report abstention
precision and recall separately. One cheap A/B rides along: the same cell with
and without an explicit "answer INSUFFICIENT if the source does not support
it" clause, which settles whether abstention is promptable at all.

**Item 5 — deadline and throughput.** pass@deadline at 60/120/300/900 s is
*derived* from the `wall_s` already recorded on every trial and costs no extra
GPU; v5 produced two correct answers at 1,457 s and 1,780 s that a pass rate
hid. One dedicated cell measures the batch lane: fifty small jobs
back-to-back, reporting items per hour at accuracy over threshold, and the
load/unload overhead separately.

**Item 6 — escalation economics.** Three families from item 3 run two arms: a
hosted-only arm, and a local-draft-then-hosted-verify arm. Recorded: hosted
input and output tokens both ways, and net correctness. A local draft that is
wrong in a plausible way can cost more hosted tokens than no draft, and that
result kills a use. GPU cost is the local drafts only. This also builds the
per-task cost figure §7 of the workhorse plan says does not exist.

## 4. Gates, all CPU and all before any GPU spend

Carried forward unchanged from v7 authoring: reference passes full score; an
untouched sandbox is a clean `visibly_failed`; a plausible wrong answer is
`confidently_wrong`; the six shaped near-misses must not change a correct
verdict; `probe_idempotence` grades twice for the same verdict.

Added for v8's new grading shapes, because a checker stricter than its prompt
manufactures the headline number
(`org/local-llm-bench-desaturation-2026-09-05.md`):

- **Two-directional instrument proof.** A synthetic perfect answer must score
  1.0 and a synthetic answer carrying every decoy must score 0. An instrument
  that has only been shown to pass a good answer is not trusted.
- **Occupancy validity, not difficulty.** `peak_prompt` is recorded per trial
  and a cell is **void** if it misses its rung by more than 15%. This is a
  validity condition — did the cell test what it claims — and explicitly not a
  difficulty gate, so it does not fall under the coverage-gate ruling that
  "nothing becomes a gate again until it has been shown to correlate with
  difficulty in the right direction" (`decisions-r4-2026-09-06.md`).

## 5. Operating rules

- **n=10 per cell minimum.** All of v7's discrimination lived at n=10; the
  20-slot single-trial pass was noise (`campaign-STATUS.md`: 5/10, 5/10, 7/10,
  8/10, 9/10, 10/10).
- One scored model, `q27-IQ2_M-96k`. A second quant only from reserve.
- Reference arms from families other than the authoring one, with a
  desaturation target: reference pooled at or below 95%, else the cell is
  reported as ceilinged.
- Bands and rungs are reported separately and never pooled.
- Verify the GPU with a real load reporting a processor split and a throughput
  on the known curve, never with a version; unload with `keep_alive: 0` before
  any direct `llama-server` probe; `/api/ps` empty at the end of every round.
- Every GPU-holding interval appended to `results/v8/GPU_BUDGET.log`.
- Every trial records `wall_s`, stop reason, `peak_prompt`, and output tokens.
- The desktop is the owner's machine; the availability rule governs.

## 6. Sequence

Phase 0, authoring and runners — no GPU. Phase 1, CPU gates — no GPU; nothing
proceeds on a cell whose instrument has not passed both directions. Phase 2,
GPU rounds in ascending cost, cheapest validity cells first so a harness bug
is found before a heavy cell pays for it. Phase 3, analysis, per-use go/no-go,
and the cost ledger.

## 7. Banked for the owner

   (long serial state, large correct output) are reserve item (c).
2. Item 2 re-opens prompt-side material under a load-bearing definition; §3
   states the distinction.
