campaign. Large is the seven-task holdout (`g01,g03,g04,t01,t02,t03,t04`). Means are per cell;
all figures below come from the finished arm JSON files.

## Plain answer

At high on tiny, Hlineno beats baseline on pass rate (23/24 vs 22/24) and confidently-wrong
count (1 vs 2). It does not beat baseline on cost: Hlineno uses 45,320 mean input and 3,400
mean output tokens versus 32,648 and 3,589; its combined token total is higher (48,720 vs
36,237), despite lower output tokens and wall time (93 s vs 98 s).

At high on large, Hlineno does not beat baseline on pass rate (both 7/7) or confidently-wrong
count (both 0), and it does not beat baseline on cost: Hlineno uses 306,128 mean input and
8,861 mean output tokens versus 176,829 and 6,129, with 251 s versus 171 s mean wall.

## Ranking at high

- Tiny: 1. Hlineno (23 passes, 1 confidently wrong, 3,400 mean output tokens); 2. baseline
  (22, 2, 3,589).
- Large: 1. baseline (7 passes, 0 confidently wrong, 6,129 mean output tokens); 2. Hlineno
  (7, 0, 8,861).
