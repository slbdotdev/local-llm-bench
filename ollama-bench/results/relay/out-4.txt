Reading: a 2.5x gen gap and 3-5x prompt gap between quants that differ by 6% in file size is the CPU-offload penalty. There is no knee inside the curve; the offloaded quants are slow from 0k and degrade further with fill, the resident ones stay flat to 27k. So "Q3_K_S fastest at equal accuracy" is a statement about the 16 GB card, not about the quant.
Practical conclusion for this GPU at 32k ctx: Q3_K_S is the largest quant that stays fully resident, so it is the right local default; Q3_K_M only makes sense if you accept ~2.5x slower generation.
