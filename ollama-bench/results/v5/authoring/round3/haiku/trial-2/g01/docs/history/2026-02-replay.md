# 2026-02 replay findings

Operations replay exposed a subtle difference between a rejected change and a
zero contribution. `ignore` and `void` are rejected, while `hold` is accepted
with multiplier zero. A hold is therefore a real occurrence and can create an
entry in an otherwise empty source. It also contributes its accepted labels.

Arithmetic is always signed multiplication. `remove` has multiplier -1, so a
raw delta of -4 contributes +4. The reducer does not infer whether a change is
valid from the sign or truthiness of its delta. A zero add, zero remove, or
zero adjust remains an accepted occurrence.

The replay team requested that rejection happen before key and label work. This
keeps administrative labels out of later entries and avoids reserving a key
position for something that is not an observation. The source bucket exception
remains: source lifecycle happens before rejection.
