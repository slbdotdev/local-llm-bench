What is being measured and why this variant is needed. The suite grades quantized local 27B
models against hosted reference models. Two properties of the current suite make it unable to
separate one model from another, and this variant fixes both at once:

- **It is too easy.** Every reference model passes t02's current variants on essentially every
  trial, so the task carries no information about which model is better.
- **Its material is far too small.** The measured seed material of `cand-2` is about
  301 tokens. The campaign's question is how much real material a local model can be
  handed in one go, and a task with 301 tokens of material cannot answer it.

Your deliverable: `ollama-bench/tasks-v5/t02/cand-5/`, a complete task directory per
CONTRACT.md section "Where your task goes" plus amendment A8.

Hard requirements, all of them:

- **Band: large.** The seed material must measure **30,000 to 45,000 tokens** by the exact
  character-count method in amendment A2, at 4.664 characters per token. Measure it with the
  snippet in A2; do not estimate. Write `MANIFEST.json` with the measured numbers.
- **The material must be material the task genuinely requires** — more real sources to reconcile,
  more modules in the tree, a longer history to search. Not filler. If the task does not need it,
  it does not belong in it. Grow the question, not the padding.
- **The reference solution stays under 5,000 output tokens** (~200 lines of Python) and the whole
  job stays doable in under 300 seconds by a model generating at ~45 tokens/second. Growing the
  reading while holding the writing constant is the entire point.
- `prompt.md` stays well under 2,000 words and must not name the single file holding the answer
  for a comprehension task.
- Standard library Python only, deterministic, no network, no pip.
- The checker follows CONTRACT.md's checker protocol exactly, including the 45-second watchdog and
  the `VERDICT correct|visibly_failed|confidently_wrong` line.
- **Amendment A7 is mandatory**: run your checker against the six shaped near-misses A7 lists,
  plus one plausible-but-wrong answer and one untouched sandbox, and record every outcome in
  `NOTES.md`. Any near-miss in A7 items 1-5 that fails is a defect in your checker — the prompt
  does not specify trailing newlines, blank lines, line endings or trailing spaces, so the checker
  must not depend on them. Fix it and re-run.
- Difficulty comes from amendment A4's first three levers only: more material to hold at once, a
  plausible-looking answer that is wrong, and more steps that must each be right in series.
  **Never from ambiguity.** A task must be hard to do and never hard to understand. If two careful
  readers could disagree about what the prompt asks, it is broken.
- State plainly in `NOTES.md` whether the correct answer to your variant is positive or negative
  (amendment A6).
