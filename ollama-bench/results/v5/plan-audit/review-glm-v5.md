## Verdict

The plan's two foundations are sound — the §1 keep/drop/rework calls match runs.jsonl exactly (45 usable runs, costs to the cent), and §7a is genuinely proven: the clamp arithmetic reproduces to Δ0/+34/−20 against the cited source, and the harness-not-model footnote is justified. But the campaign should **not start as written**: the local arm is correctly blocked on H1/H2/H4/H5 but §8's "identical 64k config" is impossible for Q3_K_M per the plan's own gpu-tune summary; "a Unity task cannot run locally at 32k at all" is contradicted by the plan's own scenario D plus H10 (subset-at-the-gateway makes it survivable, and the scenario table omits the 18 KB bench CLAUDE.md that the real contestant receives); §4 violates §3's own check-count/weight rules on nearly every task and hands P3's largest credit to a no-op; the Sonnet feasibility gate as written will bounce the suite's hardest candidates while its Haiku gate can pass a near-saturated one; the gate budget is anchored on a blended mean that new build-shaped Unity tasks will likely exceed by 1.5-2.5×; the Blender deny that makes the containment story true is still a promise, with two `blender-mcp` bridges running right now and no runtime protection for D:\avatars against a headless-Blender script; and several figures headed for the owner's report (model means, 55.3 tool calls, schema bytes) are not reproducible from their cited artifacts. Apply the fixes above — all bench-local, none needing a commit or fleet change — start authoring with P1/U4/U2 and the corrected §1 aggregates, land the blender deny and the per-check trap-flip rule before the first gate, and re-run the 64k decision per-quant; with those in place the campaign is worth starting, because what is wrong with it is wording and gating, not the underlying measurements or the task design.
