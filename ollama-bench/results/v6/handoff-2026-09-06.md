## What the campaign did, and under what harness

**Every scored row ran under the new pi harness**, proven before the first one and in both
directions (D6-4):

- negative control, `PIBENCH_PI_ARGS` pointed at a path that does not exist: the trial died in
  **0.4 s**, rc 1, `Error: Failed to load extension …`. Artifact
  `results/v6-hookproof-bad.json`.
- positive control, the deployed path: g01 PASS, `correct`, 183.9 s, 12 turns, 11 tool calls.
  Artifact `results/v6-hookproof-good.json`.

So `PIBENCH_PI_ARGS` does cross `WSLENV` into the Windows process and does reach pi's argv, and
no v6 row can have run without the extension: `runcell.sh` is the single entry point for every
scored cell and carries both exports itself.

**The GPU was verified by a real load before the first scored trial** (D6-3), never by a version
string: `gpu_verify.py q27-Q2_K_L-24k` returned **58.35 gen tok/s at 100% GPU**, against
Q2_K_L's known curve of 58 empty. Daemon: Ollama 0.33.3 on Windows, `OLLAMA_FLASH_ATTENTION=1`,
`OLLAMA_KV_CACHE_TYPE=q8_0` — the machine's own settings, not a lever in this campaign.

**The placement instrument agrees with v5's to the decimal.** Q2_K_L at 64k measured 13.35 GB
resident, 100% GPU, `nvidia-smi` peak 15,118 MiB, against v5's 13.35 GB and 15,152 MiB; Q2_K_L
at 24k measured 11.83 GB against v5's 11.83. Every v6 number can be read beside a v5 one.
