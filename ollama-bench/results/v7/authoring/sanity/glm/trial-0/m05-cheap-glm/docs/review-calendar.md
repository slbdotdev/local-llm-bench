# Pricing review calendar

Standing meetings that touch the card or the calculator:

- **Quarterly pricing review** - Compliance Review chairs; re-reads the
  card and the calculator side by side, rule by rule. This is the only
  forum where the card changes.
- **Monthly claims walk** - Client Integrations; walks the disputed list
  (samples/disputed-2026-09.md is the September one) and checks whether
  any dispute was a calculator problem rather than a depot-input
  problem.
- **Depot briefing** - after every card change; the worked examples are
  read out to the counters, because the counters sanity-check against
  them.

Outcomes land in history/ as dated records. A rule that changed without
a dated record is treated as not changed.
