# 2026-04-29 - S-1 payroll query, resolved

Finance queried the S-1 (payroll clearing) balance for March 2026:
their figure and the ledger's differed by 460.00 exactly.

Found: a wage bill of 460.00 dated 2026-03-31 had been raised, found
wrong the same day, and reversed - but the reversal pair had been keyed
against the wrong month's working copy, so both lines sat in the April
draft while Finance had March's file.

Resolution: the pair was re-keyed into March's file as it closed, the
April draft was corrected, and the figures agreed. Finance confirmed in
writing on 2026-05-02; the query is closed.

What it changed: nothing in the rules. It is recorded because it is the
worked example of reconciliation rule 3 - the pair counts as nothing
wherever it sits, and a figure that counts one leg of a pair will be
wrong by exactly that leg. S-1 has been quiet since; K-7's September
query is the first one since.
