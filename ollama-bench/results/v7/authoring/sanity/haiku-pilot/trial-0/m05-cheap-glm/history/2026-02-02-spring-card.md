# 2026-02-02 - spring card issued

The spring card was issued on 2026-02-02 and was in force until the May
review amended parts of it. What it introduced:

- the Saturday delivery fee, set at 9.00 flat;
- the oversize supplement, at the 25 kg threshold with a 14.00 amount;
- the returns label as a separate flat product rather than a manual
  adjustment.

The spring card's own text is kept under history/superseded/ because
claims older than September are settled on the card in force at the
time. Its divisor wording was one prose sentence and one example, which
did not say the same thing; the September card states the divisor once
(R2) precisely so that cannot recur.
