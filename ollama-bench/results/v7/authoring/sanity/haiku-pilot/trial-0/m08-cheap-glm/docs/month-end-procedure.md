# Month-end procedure

Owner: Compliance Review. How a month's CSV comes to exist and closes.

1. Through the month, rows are drafted from the invoice folder and the
   till roll in date order. A row is added once and never edited; a
   mistake is reversed with a REVERSED/REVERSAL pair, which is why the
   pair exists at all.
2. At the close, the drafter re-adds the month by hand: file total per
   account against the invoice folder, then the till. A mismatch stops
   the close until it is explained.
3. The closed file gets no further rows. Anything after the close waits
   for the next month's file, whatever its date - late paperwork is
   entered late, on purpose, so a file is always internally complete.
4. The quarter close (September) is also when the year-end checklist is
   reopened and the OPENING agreement is re-run for the next year.

The procedure deliberately produces files a person can verify alone with
a pencil: three files, whole, per the reconciliation rules. Anything
that cannot be checked with a pencil is not a number this ledger
publishes.
