Counter queries - scratch notes, answers given in person

*(M. Lindqvist. Questions the counters asked at handover; kept here so
the answers stay put. Nothing here changes any rule; the policy doc is
where rules live.)*

Q. The castors show 44 on the shelf and the point is 45 - is that a
reorder or not?
A. 44 is below 45, so yes, it is on the list. Below the point always is.

Q. And if the shelf showed exactly 45?
A. Then it is also on the list. The point is the level you reorder AT,
not a line you fall through. The policy doc says this in those words.

Q. The August file shows 49 for castors. Which figure do we quote?
A. The current file. The August sheet was one Friday; the current file is
the shelves as they are.

Q. A new line came in that has no point configured at all.
A. It stays off the low-stock check until the review gives it a point
(policy rule 4). Do not guess one.
