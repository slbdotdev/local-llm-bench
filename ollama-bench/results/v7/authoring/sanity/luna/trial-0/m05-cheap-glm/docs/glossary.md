# Glossary - pricing

- **Billable weight** - the weight the base charge is computed on: the
  larger of the actual weight and the volumetric weight, both whole
  kilograms (R2).
- **Volumetric weight** - length x width x height in centimetres divided
  by 5000, rounded up to the next whole kilogram.
- **Zone factor** - the R3 multiplier for the parcel's zone letter.
- **Fuel line** - the fuel surcharge amount: 12% of the zone-multiplied
  base charge, and nothing else (R5).
- **Supplement** - a flat amount added for a property of the delivery:
  residential (R4), oversize (R7), Saturday (R6). No supplement is ever
  part of the fuel line.
- **Returns label** - a flat 5.80, zone A only, refused elsewhere (R8).
- **Base charge** - the R1 amount for the billable weight, before any
  multiplier or supplement.
- **Consignment** - one or more parcels moving together. The calculator
  prices one parcel; a consignment's price is the sum of its parcels.

If a word is used in two ways anywhere in the tree, the rate card's way
wins; that is what "the card is the specification" means.
