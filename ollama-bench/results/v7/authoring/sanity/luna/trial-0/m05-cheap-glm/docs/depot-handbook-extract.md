# Depot handbook extract - taking a quote

*(Extract reproduced for the engineering tree so the figures asked for
match the calculator's inputs. Client Integrations owns the original.)*

When a counter clerk quotes a parcel over the phone, they ask for, in
this order:

1. the actual weight in whole kilograms, from the scales;
2. the length, width and height in centimetres, longest side first;
3. the destination country, which gives the zone letter;
4. whether the delivery address is a residence;
5. whether a Saturday delivery is wanted.

They then read back the price the calculator gives. They do not compute
anything in their head; the calculator is the only quoting device at the
counter. If the caller disputes the figure, the clerk does not argue:
the dispute goes to Client Integrations under the claims process, and
the depot recompute is done from the rate card figures for the same
parcel facts.

A returns label is quoted only when the destination is zone A. For any
other destination the clerk refuses politely and offers a forwarded
quote from the depot manager instead.
