# Kestrel Courier - rate card

**SUPERSEDED. In force 2026-02-02 to 2026-08-31 only. The current card
is docs/rate-card.md; nothing on this page prices anything today. Kept
only because claims older than September are settled on the card that
was in force (see docs/claims-process.md, step 4).**

All amounts are euros. Rounding: to the nearest cent, halves up.

## Base charge by billable weight

- up to and including 1 kg: 6.00
- over 1 kg up to and including 5 kg: 6.00 plus 1.80 for each whole
  kilogram over 1
- over 5 kg: 13.20 plus 1.10 for each whole kilogram over 5

## Billable weight

The volumetric weight is length x width x height in centimetres divided
by the volumetric divisor. Billable weight is the larger of the actual
weight and the volumetric weight, in whole kilograms. (For the avoidance
of doubt on old claims: the divisor stated in this paragraph is the
volumetric divisor, and the worked examples below are consistent with
it.)

## Zone multiplier

A 1.00, B 1.15, C 1.25, D 1.50, applied to the base charge.

## Supplements

- residential address: a flat 4.00, after the fuel line.
- Saturday delivery: a flat 8.00, after the fuel line.
- billable weight over 25 kg: a flat 12.00, after the fuel line.

## Fuel surcharge

10% of the zone-multiplied base charge.

## Returns label

Flat 6.00, zone A only.
