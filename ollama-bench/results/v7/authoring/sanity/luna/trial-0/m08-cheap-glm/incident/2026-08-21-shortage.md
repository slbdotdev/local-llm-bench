# Incident: crate count short at Towpath warehouse

*Raised 2026-08-21 by the depot counter. Status: OPEN, paused.*

## What happened

The Friday count on 2026-08-21 found three crates of rope fasteners fewer
than the stock system expected at the Towpath warehouse. The system figure
has been steady all month; the count has come in short twice running.

## What is known

- The stock system figure is derived from delivery notes, not from the
  ledger CSVs in this repository; the two systems do not talk.
- No delivery note is missing: every inbound crate since 2026-08-01 has a
  signed note.
- No customer or counter sale explains the gap; counter sales are rung
  through the till and the till closed balanced on both Fridays.

## What is not known

- Whether the crates left, were mis-shelved, or were never received as
  three crates rather than two.
- Where the transfer went, if there was a transfer.

## Paused, and why

The scanner export that would show shelf movements for August was
requested from IT on 2026-08-24 (ticket 8841) and has not arrived. The
investigation is paused until it does.

## Effect on the books

None. This is a physical-stock question. No ledger entry has been raised,
none is to be inferred from this file, and the ledger is reconciled
independently of the stock system (docs/reconciliation-rules.md, rule 6).
