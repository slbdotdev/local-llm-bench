# 2026-02-01 - ledger practice set up

The practice Compliance Review put in place when the canal shop took
over the wholesale counter, all still in force:

- Monthly CSV files, one per calendar month, rows in date order, never
  edited after close (docs/month-end-procedure.md).
- Five accounts, K-7 K-9 M-2 M-5 S-1, codes fixed and documented
  (docs/account-map.md).
- Errors handled only by REVERSED/REVERSAL pairs; no line is ever
  corrected in place (reconciliation rules, rule 3).
- Balances computed from the OPENING row plus counted rows; physical
  matters never enter the books unrecorded (rule 6).

Nothing here has needed to change since. The stock-system migration in
the README's TODO is about where the rows are keyed, not about any of
these rules.
