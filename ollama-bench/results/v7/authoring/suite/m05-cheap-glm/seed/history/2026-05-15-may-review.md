# 2026-05-15 - May pricing review

Decisions of the May review, which the current zone factors come from:

- Zone factors set to the values still in force: A 1.00, B 1.15, C 1.30,
  D 1.55. The review considered a fifth zone for the Nordic routes and
  rejected it; SE, FI, NO and PL stay in zone D until further review.
- The fuel surcharge moved from a tiered percentage to a flat 12%.
  Minutes note the intent: one fuel line, computed on the priced base,
  never on the flat amounts.
- No change to the Saturday fee or the oversize rules; those date from
  the spring card and carried over.

Effective 2026-05-15. Superseded only where the September card restates
a rule; see 2026-09-01-rate-card-effective.md.
