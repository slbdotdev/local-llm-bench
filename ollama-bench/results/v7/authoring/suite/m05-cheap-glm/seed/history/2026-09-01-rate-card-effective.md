# 2026-09-01 - September rate card effective

The 2026-09-01 card (docs/rate-card.md) replaced the spring card. Changes
agreed at the August pricing review:

- the fuel surcharge is restated as 12% of the zone-multiplied base
  charge alone, so that no supplement is ever part of the fuel line (R5);
- the volumetric divisor is restated as 5000 for all services (R2);
- returns labels confirmed zone A only (R8), refunds handled under the
  claims process.

Zone factors are unchanged since the May review. The worked examples were
re-checked by hand against the card on 2026-08-29.
