# Capacity Planning: the limits report

## What the report is

The limits report is Capacity Planning's own working spreadsheet. It is regenerated from the
manifest whenever the manifest changes, and it is the document the quarterly capacity review is
argued from, so what it does and does not cover is worth stating in one place.

## What it covers

**The report covers the stages Capacity Planning is accountable for, which is every stage in the
manifest except the ones on Platform Reliability's rota.** Platform Reliability owns the capacity
of the stages it carries the pager for; their limits are argued in that group's own review and
are not Capacity Planning's to plan, so a row for one of them in this report has twice led to the
same number being changed in two places by two teams in one quarter.

Which stages are on that rota is recorded in exactly one place, the on-call column of
`docs/operations.md`. It is not in the manifest, deliberately: the manifest describes what the
stages *are*, and a rota is a fact about people, which changes on a different clock and belongs
with the operational material.

## What it does not do

The report carries no owner, no on-call team and no commentary. It is three columns and it is
sorted for reading, and anything else in it is something a reviewer will argue about instead of
arguing about capacity.
