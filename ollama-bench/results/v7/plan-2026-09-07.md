## 1. What calibration proved

1.1 The workhorse scored 19 of 20 correct, 95%, before tuning (D7-34), and both neighbours scored
within one trial of it, UDQ3KXL 20/20 and Q2_K 19/20, with the main band 10/10 for all three
quants. The two misses are on different rows, IQ2_M's `m03-cheap-claude` against Q2_K's
`m10-cheap-luna` with UDQ3KXL missing neither, and two single misses on disjoint rows is trial
noise rather than a difficulty gradient (D7-41). The median main-band trial is 8 turns, 10 tool
calls and 4 file reads against a tree of 91 to 95 files, and achieved occupancy runs 4 to 27% of
the window and 9 to 55% of the material, so no row reached half the occupancy the band was authored
for and the median reached a quarter of it (D7-32, calibration section 1). `m09-main-glm`'s
first-pass run carries a `tools` histogram of nine `bash` calls, one `write` and no `read`
(`results/v7cal-IQ2_M-main.json`), so a `read`-only count undercounts traversal.

1.2 Two tasks were hardened by the v5 ladder and neither moved the pass rate (D7-37, D7-39), though
`m09-main-glm`'s achieved occupancy rose from 26% to 33% of its material, the only quantitative
evidence a hardening of this kind gives (D7-38). `m06-main-glm` is labelled `short-traversal`
rather than hardened, because mode 6's traceback names the file holding the defect (D7-34). Three
further main-band rows came back `unsafe` at a perfect score with an empty note list, and those are
a grader defect rather than a result: `os.path.normcase` was applied to one side of a file-set
comparison and it lowercases on Windows, so the gate fired on every sandbox including each
reference's, with 20 of 20 references correct under `python3` and 17 under the Windows interpreter
(D7-31, calibration section 5).

1.3 **This suite measures traversal-shy solving.** The material is on disk, genuinely required and
not recognised as filler, and the model sets it aside by never opening it, because an agentic model
with a file reader and `grep` answers from the two files that carry the answer (D7-32). **The quant
is not the variable**, since three quants spanning this card's viable range clear the main band
outright (D7-41). **The 50% gap is a property of the tasks**: the authoring brief's acceptance rule
tested that no single grep token finds the answer, never that targeted reads cannot assemble it.

## 2. The new acceptance rule, and the test that enforces it

2.1 **The rule.** A main-band task's answer requires reconciling facts from several files that the
prompt's own vocabulary cannot locate. No single file assembles it, and no grep over the prompt's
own words assembles it either.

2.2 **The gate is a pair, and both halves must pass.** Run the candidate on the workhorse,
`q27-IQ2_M-64k`, one trial, before it enters the suite. **Material coverage must reach 50% of the
candidate's material**, and the trial must touch at least **five** of the paths the checker names
load-bearing. Peak input is reported beside both as the capacity number and is never itself a gate,
because `achieved_fill_prompt_tokens` includes the system prompt, the task, prior turns and tool
results (calibration section 11), so it is an upper bound on material read. **Material coverage is
the attributable number**: with `read_paths` it is the sum of `material_tokens` for the files in
`read_paths` that the manifest lists, over the candidate's own `material_tokens`.

2.3 **Why 50%.** v6's D6-30 measured the only two rows in this toolchain that ever worked a band
honestly, `g03` at 25,748 of 30,018 tokens of material (86%) and `t03` at 16,502 of 30,604 (54%),
and both clear 50% on the peak-input proxy, so the fraction rests on measured precedent, and
applying it to coverage makes it harder still. v7's best main-band row reached 55% and its median
24% (calibration section 1), so 50% fails nine of the ten current tasks and passes the one that
came closest, which is the discrimination a gate needs. Half the material is 15,500 to 18,000
tokens, 24 to 28% of the 64k window, so v5 section 3.3's working margin survives.

2.4 **The file gate, and the honest status of its numbers.** Each main-band `test.py` declares
`LOAD_BEARING`, at least six seed paths, each annotated with the causal hop it carries and at least
three distinct hops represented; the acceptance trial must touch at least five. **Six, three and
five are proposed values and no measurement backs them**, because neither the v5 large-band rows
nor v6's D6-30 recorded how many distinct files a trial opened and pibench has never had a field
that could. **Re-deriving all three counts from the first sweep's measured `read_paths`
distributions is the round's first calibration item.**

2.5 **The fields these numbers come from.** Peak input is `achieved_fill_prompt_tokens` in each run
record of `results/v7cal-<quant>-<band>.json` under `<tag>.runs[]`, never `in_tokens`, which sums
across turns and overstates occupancy several-fold (calibration section 11). Material is
`material_tokens` in `MANIFEST.json`. `turns` counts assistant messages that reached `message_end`,
`tool_calls` counts `tool_execution_start` events, `tools` is the per-tool histogram, and
`timed_out` and `stop_reason` are reported beside every verdict (5.1). **Two additions have to be
made, and the gate works without neither.** First, `MANIFEST.json` carries only `material_chars`,
`material_tokens`, `chars_per_token` and a `seed_files` count, so **add `files`, a map of seed path
to token count**, written by `authoring/measure_material.py` at the suite's own 4.664 chars per
token and re-stamped by `stamp_manifests.py`; coverage cannot be computed without it. Second,
pibench records `tools` as a name-to-count histogram and keeps no path, so no field carries file
identity (`pibench.py`, `tool_execution_start` handling). **Add `read_paths`**, the sorted set of
distinct paths seen on `tool_execution_start`, its argument key read off one live event stream
before the round starts and never guessed. It covers `bash` as well as `read`, since 1.2 shows a
model traverses by `cat` and `grep` as readily as by `read`, and a `bash` command contributes a
path by a token scan of its command string against the manifest's file list. Every path is
normcased and made relative to the sandbox root, per 4.1. Before any candidate is gated on it, one
validation run reproduces a cell of `results/v7cal-IQ2_M-main.json` and checks the new field
against that record's `tools` histogram; the stored records hold no raw events, so the check is a
re-run and not a re-parse. **Failing either half of the gate is a re-author, not a tune.** A
candidate that misses goes back to its family once with the measured numbers, and one that misses
twice is dropped for the family's spare. This is not selection under rule 4a: the gate is read
before any verdict.

## 4. Grader rules earned this campaign

4.1 **Grade under the interpreter pibench grades with**,
`PY=/mnt/c/Users/slb/scoop/apps/python/current/python.exe`, never `python3` for a grader verdict;
`"$PY" results/v7/probe_scope_gate.py <slot>` runs after every grader change and `--breach` proves
the gate still fires on a real breach (D7-31). **Grade every candidate twice and require the same
answer**, with `probe_idempotence.py`, since a grader may run the solver's work and must put back
every file it disturbs (D7-15). **`os.path.normcase` both sides of any path comparison**, and print
the difference rather than a bare boolean, `SCOPE created [...]; missing [...]`, because `VERDICT
unsafe` beside `FAIL []` is a verdict nobody can adjudicate from the artifact (D7-31). **Mode 8's
budget is measured in the bench's own fields**, applied by the manager at analysis time, because
the grader cannot see them by design (D7-36). **The declared turn number in the current tasks is
wrong** (D7-40): `m08-main-luna` declares 5 turns and measured 9, 7, 7 and 7 across four trials
while meeting its 900-token output ceiling on three of four. **`turns` and `tool_calls` are
separate fields and neither defines the other**: pibench increments `turns` on an assistant
`message_end` and `tool_calls` on a `tool_execution_start`, so a turn is one assistant message,
which on this suite usually carries one tool call but is not the same quantity. Those four trials
measured `tool_calls` of 10, 8, 8 and 9 and `out_tokens` of 1,267, 681, 855 and 676
(`results/v7cal-IQ2_M-main.json`, `results/v7cal2-IQ2_M-main.json`). **The corrected rule: a mode-8
budget is a `tool_calls` ceiling and an `out_tokens` ceiling, never a turn count**,. Each ceiling
is re-derived from the reference arms' measured median plus 50%, `turns` is a diagnostic and never
a budget, every mode-8 row reports both ceilings beside the verdict whether or not either is
breached, and the numbers stay the author's to set (D7-40).

## 6. Standing rules

6.1 Everything in `plan-2026-09-06.md` section 8 carries forward in full and is not restated here,
from the outcome-file polling rule to the never-touch list. Added to it: **launch chains and worker
runs detached, and poll with a Monitor.** This harness kills long-lived background tasks on a
spurious low-memory signal at 80 to 90 s, so a chain is `setsid -f bash <script> < /dev/null >
<log> 2>&1`, every `codex-run` or `pi-run` goes the same way, and a persistent Monitor polls a
marker file (`manager-brief-calibration-2026-09-06.md` section 4). **bake the 24k tags rather than
passing `num_ctx`**, because the context window lives in the tag and pibench's `--num-ctx` feeds
only its tps probe, which `--no-tps` disables (same brief, section 1, D7-27). **Verify the GPU by a
real load**, never by a version string: 100% GPU, residency on the placement table, generation on
the quant's known curve (D7-28). **Sandboxes outside every checkout**: pibench satisfies this by
construction in the Windows temp directory (D7-29), `sanity.py` does not, and `V7_SANDBOX_ROOT` is
set before any arm. **Pause the plan window at 80%**, numbers only, key never printed. **No
heredocs and no inline multi-line programs**: write the file, run the file. Never `pgrep -f`.

## What changed against `plan-2026-09-06.md`, and why

The old plan sought difficulty inside each task and gave the v5 ladder to find it; calibration
proved that ladder cannot reach the target, because two hardenings moved the pass rate by nothing
and the gap is nine tasks (D7-34, D7-37). The measured cause is that the main band is never
occupied, 4 to 27% of the window and 9 to 55% of the material on a median of 4 file reads out of a
91 to 95 file tree, (D7-32, D7-41). This revision adds one property to the acceptance rule, that a
main-band answer must reconcile facts the prompt's vocabulary cannot locate, and one acceptance
test that can actually fail: a material-coverage and load-bearing-file gate read from the bench's
own fields, with peak input demoted to a capacity diagnostic beside it. It puts a rung 0, "make the
material necessary", ahead of v5's three rungs, re-authors nine main-band slots on that axis with
the public benchmark shapes named per mode in 3.2's table, keeps mode 6 and the cheap band exempt
for structural reasons, and carries the old section 8 forward with the rules the campaign paid for:
the Windows interpreter for graders, the detach-and-Monitor launch, the baked context tag, and mode
8's budget in tool calls rather than turns.
