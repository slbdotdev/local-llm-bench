## The calibration — run 2026-09-06, and its result

*This section said "for tomorrow" until the run happened. It is now what happened; the full report
is `calibration-2026-09-06.md`.*

The rung was confirmed as the control session named it: **IQ2_M at its own rung, 64k**, with
**UDQ3KXL at 48k** and **Q2_K at 64k** as neighbours. The suite stayed at 48k main / 24k cheap and
**nothing was re-authored for the rung**; the occupancy figure is restated as **45-56%** at 64k,
exactly as D7-1 said it would be.

**Headline: 19 of 20 correct, 95%, on the workhorse before tuning.** One `confidently_wrong`, zero
`unsafe`, zero `unverified_claim`, zero `visibly_failed`. The target was about 50%.

Two tasks were hardened by the v5 ladder — `m09-main-glm` and `m05-cheap-glm`, both from the
hardening list drawn up on authoring night, both blind-reviewed and accepted — and `m06-main-glm`
was labelled `short-traversal` rather than hardened. **Calibration was not selection**: no task was
kept, dropped, reworded or reordered because a quant passed or failed it.

**The neighbours were measured beside it and do not separate:** UDQ3KXL at 48k scored **20/20**,
Q2_K at 64k scored **19/20**, IQ2_M **19/20** — and the two misses are on *different* rows
(`m03-cheap-claude` for IQ2_M, `m10-cheap-luna` for Q2_K). Three quants spanning the viable range
of this card land within one trial of each other, so the gap to the 50% target is a property of the
tasks and not of the quant they were measured on (D7-41).

The finding that matters more than the rate is that **the main band is not occupied**: 4-27% of the
window and 9-55% of the material, measured per trial, and the neighbours reproduce the same range
row for row. See "What is unfinished", item 1.

After the two hardenings, the tasks that changed were re-run three trials each on the workhorse:
**15 of 15 `correct`**, with `m09-main-glm`'s achieved occupancy up from 26% to 33% of its material
(D7-38) and `m05-cheap-glm`'s restored sixth subcheck scored correctly at 6/6 (D7-39).

## The three most surprising things

**1. Every fairness defect that mattered was found by a second model doing the task, not by a
checker.** Four defects survived the reference, sandbox, and perturbation checks:

| slot | defect | the instrument that found it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| m01-main-claude | `active_count()` underspecified; the material stated half the rule | Haiku disagreeing with my reference and being right |
| m04-main-claude | the test invocation was never stated; the grader supplied it silently | both arms restructuring the tree to make the tests runnable — one added a `.venv`, one moved the package to the root |
| m02-main-luna | the grader required backticks around a table key the prompt never asked for | Sonnet and Haiku writing the same correct answer in two house styles |
| m05-cheap-glm | a subcheck scored a *classification* the material could not settle | two Claude arms giving one non-reference attribution, then Luna giving a third |

Rule: **a task is not finished when its grader is clean; it is finished when a reader who is not
its author has done it.**

**2. A grader ate the answer it was grading:** `m10-main-claude` went from 9/9 `correct` to 8/9
`confidently_wrong` on a second grading because it restored the manifest but not the regenerated CSV.
`probe_idempotence.py` now grades twice; graders must restore every file they disturb.

**3. Two of three families independently wrote prompts that gave away the answer:** GLM found
`m10-main-claude`, `m02-main-luna`, and `m01-cheap-luna` answerable from prompt-visible facts.
This has no checker, probe, or reference-arm signal; it justifies the roundtable.

## What is unfinished

*Rewritten 2026-09-06 after the calibration run on the GPU. The result is in
`calibration-2026-09-06.md`; the reasoning is in `decisions.md` as D7-26 … D7-38. Items 1, 2 and 6
of the previous list are closed and are not repeated here.*

**Calibration is done.** The workhorse is **IQ2_M at 64k**, verified by load at 100% GPU, with
**UDQ3KXL at 48k** and **Q2_K at 64k** beside it. The suite scored **19 of 20 correct — 95%** on
the workhorse before tuning, against a target of about 50%. Two tasks were hardened by the v5
ladder and blind-reviewed; `m06-main-glm` is labelled `short-traversal` rather than hardened.

1. **The suite does not discriminate, and one more round of task-level tuning will not fix it.**
   Four frontier arms scored 90-100% on this suite and a 27B 2-bit quant scored 95%. The cause is
   measured rather than guessed: **the main band's achieved occupancy is 4-27% of the window and
   9-55% of the material** (D7-32). The material is on disk, is genuinely required, and is never
   read, because an agentic model answers from the two files that carry the answer. The authoring
   brief's acceptance rule tested that no *single* grep token finds the answer; it did not test
   that a *handful of targeted reads* cannot assemble it.
   **The corrective is one property added to the acceptance rule** — a main-band answer must
   require reconciling facts from several files that cannot be located from the prompt's own
   vocabulary — **and one acceptance test that can actually fail**: run the accepted candidate on
   the workhorse and require the achieved peak prompt to reach a stated fraction of the material.
   That is a re-authoring round on the ten main-band tasks. Whether to spend it is the owner's.
   Scale, from the one hardening that moved this number: `m09-main-glm` went from 26% to 33% of
   its material for one added timeline row and one rewritten paragraph (D7-38).

2. **A grader must be verified on the interpreter that will run it.** Three main-band graders
   compared a normcased file set against an un-normcased one; on Windows, where `normcase`
   lowercases, that fired on every sandbox including the reference's, and the campaign recorded
   three `unsafe` rows that were not about the quant at all (D7-31). Every checker in the toolchain
   is a file reader run from WSL as `python3`; **pibench grades under the Windows interpreter**, as
   the authoring brief says in bold and nothing enforced. `results/v7/probe_scope_gate.py` is the
   standing check and takes one command. Any future grader change re-runs it.

3. **`m05-main-luna`'s scope gate may be stricter than its prompt.** The prompt forbids *modifying*
   files and is silent on *creating* one; the gate fails any created file. One reviewer read "Edit
   only the documentation" as covering both and accepted it — one reader is not two. The gate now
   names the file it objected to, so the next occurrence is adjudicable from the artifact rather
   than guessed at. The owner's, at low cost either way.

4. **`m06-main-glm` stays labelled `short-traversal`.** Mode 6 hands the model a failing test whose
   traceback names the file holding the defect, so no rung of the ladder reaches around it. Kept
   under owner's ruling 2, since only one other task covers mode 6.

5. **The ZCode arm is still the only missing reference row.** Unchanged: `authoring/run_zcode_arm.py`
   is serial, stages Windows-visible sandboxes, gates launches at 85% via `plan-usage.py`, records
   a failed `1302` as `rate_limited`, and reads `final.txt`. `glm` and `zcode` would isolate
   harness effects, and pi's resilience extension rescued twenty of twenty saturated-window runs
   where ZCode has no equivalent.

6. **`sanity.py` still defaults its sandboxes inside the repository.** No row in the calibration is
   affected — pibench's sandboxes are `tempfile.mkdtemp` under the Windows temp directory, outside
   every checkout (D7-29) — but `V7_SANDBOX_ROOT` still has to be set before any future arm.

7. **Mode 8's turn budgets need re-deriving, and that is the author's to do.** Applied for the
   first time in any campaign: `m08-main-luna` declares 5 turns and 900 output tokens, and the
   workhorse came in at 9, 7, 7 and 7 turns across four trials with output tokens inside the
   ceiling on three of them (D7-36, D7-40). Tokens are met, turns are missed every time by the same
   margin — the signature of a stable cost, not a wandering model. The budget was written as though
   a turn were a step of reasoning; in this harness a turn is a tool call. **Recorded rather than
   edited**, because changing another family's declared budget to make a row pass is the move rule
   4a forbids. A declared budget that is only quoted when it is met is not a check; it belongs in
   every mode-8 report from now on.

8. **v7 walls are not comparable with v6's.** v6 shared the card with a second campaign from 22:44
   and measured ~6% contention; nothing else ran here, and generation came in 20-25% faster on the
   same quants (D7-28). Placement and residency reproduce v6 exactly; throughput does not.
