## What is waiting for the owner

**One decision, and it is about provenance rather than difficulty.**
`results/v7/authoring-2026-09-06.md` section 8.1 records that round two's nine candidates were
"drafted under the manager by clean-context Sonnet subagents" and that their `glm`/`luna`/`claude`
labels "are the plan's slot assignments and not a claim about which model wrote the prose";
section 8.2 records that they got **one** round of review rather than the two
`plan-r3-2026-09-06.md` section 3.1 requires. Both were left as the owner's or the control session's
call in that document and neither has been answered.

It matters twice over. The 40% family cap is computed from those labels, so a nominal label makes
the cap a nominal constraint; and no round-two candidate can be admitted until the second
cross-family review exists. This round obtained that review for the two candidates worth
admitting and both came back REVISE, so nothing was admitted on a label that has not been settled
— but the question is still open and the next round will hit it again.

The three courses: **(a)** accept the round-two labels as the plan's slot assignments and require
only the two cross-family reviews before admission, which is what the campaign has done in
practice; **(b)** re-derive the labels from what actually wrote each spec and recompute the cap,
which may put the current suite outside it; **(c)** retire the round-two nine and stop counting
them as staged. The manager's recommendation is **(a)**, with the caveat written into
`roundtable.md` beside the register so nobody reads the cap as stronger than it is.

2. **Re-author `q09-main-glm` from its own spec — it is the best material this campaign has
   produced and it was dropped on a fixable defect.** Shape A, long serial state, mode 9 main.
   **All four reference arms answered it `correct` at 12/12 and the workhorse scored 1 of 3 with
   the other two `confidently_wrong`.** Four arms right and the 2-bit model confidently wrong
   twice is the discrimination the whole suite lacks, and no other candidate in five rounds has
   shown it. Both reviewers called it fair and hard to do; one solved it from the prompt alone
   and matched `ref/`. It was dropped because two blind readers each reproduced the whole answer
   at **12/12 from two files**, twice, on two different tells.
   `r5/reviews/q09-main-glm--claude-2.md` has the three-item fix and it is precise: permute the
   log's **rows** (the chain order is currently a stride-3 reverse traversal of file position, so
   deleting the `previous` column entirely still scores 12/12) and assert at build that no affine
   or stride map from row index reproduces the chain; **delete the second `rebase`** and make
   order load-bearing with an entry kind whose applied figure is a function of the running figure
   — a `clamp` to `min(figure, basis)`, or a `relief` subtracting `min(take-back, figure)` — since
   a `rebase` *sets* and therefore truncates the chain rather than extending it; and break the
   frame, because every balance is the last line of its page and every take-back the last line of
   its module, so `tail -n1 docs/*.md src/kestrel/*.py` harvests 38 of 40 with no token at all.
   Then re-review from both families, four arms, three workhorse trials.
3. **A shape has to be enforced by the material, not stated in the brief.** Both candidates that
   reached a second review this round were dropped for the same reason and the diagnosis is the
   same in both: `q08-main-luna`'s twenty-two-step chain was readable off **one grep at full score
   with zero files opened** because the live row was identifiable without the previous step, and
   `q06-main-luna`'s thirty-two-file deliverable was writable **from one file at 39/40** because

   **assert at build time that step k's answer is not reachable without step k-1** — that no sort,
   no positional regularity, no name class and no map from row index reproduces the chain order;
   that a shuffled sum does not reproduce the final value **within every segment**, not only
   overall; and that per-entry perturbation shows each checkpoint depending on every entry before
   it. `q09`'s revision was asked for the first two and passed them, and fell to the third.
4. **`check_harvest.py`'s frame measure looks at the wrong vocabulary.** Both of this round's
